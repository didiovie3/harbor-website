Cozance Food — Milling & Distribution

Large-scale milling, agricultural trade, and efficient regional food distribution pipelines that feed growing populations.

Contact Cozance Food

Processing & Agro-Milling

We process raw grain and agricultural commodities into high-quality food ingredients and packaged staples. By partnering with outgrowers and investing in local contract farming, we secure a traceable and clean supply chain.

Operational Capacity

  • Modern Dry Milling: Multi-ton daily grain processing capacity for corn, wheat, and sorghum.
  • Agricultural Sourcing: Direct outgrower schemes connecting hundreds of local farmers to stable markets.
  • Quality Packaging: Automated bulk and retail packaging designed for extended shelf life.

Trade & Wholesale Logistics

We bridge farm-gate production with regional food markets. Cozance Food coordinates storage warehouses, merchant networks, and logistics fleets to deliver commodities safely to food retailers and industrial users.

Supply Channels

  • Commodity Trade: Sourcing, cleaning, and trading bulk grains across regional borders.
  • Warehousing: Multi-location dry-storage warehouses preserving cargo quality.
  • Merchant Supply: Reliable supply agreements with major distributors, bakeries, and retail networks.

Merchant & Supplier Inquiries

Connect with Cozance Food to explore agricultural supply contracts, distributor partnerships, bulk grain purchasing, or export sales.

Compliance & Quality Notice: Cozance Food is a registered food processing operator. Our facilities and processing protocols are aligned with NAFDAC regulatory directives, environmental health guidelines, and national seed-standards laws. We enforce strict batch testing for moisture content, purity, and aflatoxin levels at all receiving depots to ensure public safety and premium product quality.
