MWG Gas — Energy & Gas Supply

Reliable bulk gas logistics, terminal storage infrastructure, and high-performance safety standards serving industrial and commercial grids.

Contact MWG Gas

Bulk LPG Distribution

We supply Liquified Petroleum Gas (LPG) and gas products directly to manufacturing plants, power facilities, and distribution hubs. Our logistics management integrates modern transport assets with real-time tracking for reliable delivery scheduling.

Our Capabilities

  • Industrial LPG Supply: Bulk delivery agreements supporting continuous manufacturing processes.
  • Logistics Operations: High-safety tankers managed by certified hazmat logistics specialists.
  • Storage Solutions: Engineered bulk storage tanks for retail distributors and manufacturing complexes.

Safety & Compliance

Safety is the baseline of our operations. MWG Gas operates under strict HSE protocols, conducting routine pressure testing, equipment maintenance, and emergency response training to protect our personnel and host environments.

Safety Standards

  • Plant Integrity: Periodic ultrasonic testing of storage tank thickness and relief valve safety reviews.
  • Standard Certifications: Compliance with fire services directives and national environmental protection acts.
  • Handler Safety: Direct training programs for delivery crews and commercial client operators.

Industrial Supply Inquiries

Route your bulk LPG supply requests, logistics quotes, or infrastructure installation queries directly to our operations team.

Regulatory & Compliance Notice: MWG Gas operates under licensing from the Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority (NMDPRA), Federal Fire Service directives, and state environmental protection guidelines. Bulk transport operations adhere to strict weight restrictions, highway hazmat routing rules, and speed-limiter controls to guarantee public road safety and operational excellence.
