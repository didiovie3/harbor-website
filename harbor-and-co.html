Contact Harbor & Co

Bespoke Corporate Capital

We provide tailored financial structures to address liquidity requirements, trade cycles, and capital projects. Our operations are built around serving corporate and commercial entities with speed and transparency.

Our Capabilities

  • Structured Corporate Lending: Capital for capacity expansion and working capital optimization.
  • Business Consulting & Advisory: Analysis of capital structures, asset risk, and corporate development strategy.
  • Procurement & Contract Supplies: Strategic commercial supply contracts and supply chain financing.

Our Advisory Approach

We simplify financial planning by aligning with operators to evaluate growth metrics. Our four-step operational process ensures that capital is deployed where it generates high compound value.

  1. Discovery: Reviewing client balance sheets, operational goals, and cash flow hurdles.
  2. Structuring: Designing custom credit facilities or strategic advisory frameworks.
  3. Execution: Rapid legal processing and onboarding for prompt capital disbursement.
  4. Partnership: Periodic portfolio reviews to optimize corporate performance and support next-stage growth.

Connect with our Finance Team

Reach out directly to outline your corporate lending, advisory, or procurement requirements. A credit officer will review your project details.

Regulatory & Risk Disclosure: Harbor & Co is a financial services brand operating under the umbrella of Harbor Holdings Ltd. Structured lending is commercial in nature and is subject to rigorous credit committee evaluations. We do not engage in retail consumer deposit-taking activities. Consulting and procurement contracts are managed in strict accordance with national regulatory frameworks. Term loans and commercial paper interest rates fluctuate in alignment with monetary policy guidance.
